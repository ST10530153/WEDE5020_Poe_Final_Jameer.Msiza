About Nexus Tech

History

Nexus Tech opened in 2016 as a cellphone counter in a Sandton arcade. Customers started asking for laptops, honest specifications and same-week repairs, so the counter grew into a full store with a workshop bench behind the glass.

A second branch in Midrand followed in 2022 to serve customers who live or work on the N1 corridor and do not want to drive into Sandton for a battery or a screen.

Mission

To help Gauteng customers choose, buy and keep technology with confidence — by combining honest specifications, fair prices and a repair bench that outlasts the unboxing.

Vision

To be Johannesburg’s most trusted independent tech destination: the store people search when they want the right device, and the first result they open when a screen cracks.
